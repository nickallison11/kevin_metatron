# Welcome to metatron — what's live for the GTM cohort

This is what you can do on the platform today. We're letting founders and investors in through invite links so we can listen closely to early users and ship what matters most.

## If you're a founder

**Getting started**
- Click your invite link → create your account with email and password.
- Build your founder profile: company name, one-liner, stage, sector(s), country, website.
- Upload your pitch deck (PDF). Kevin reads it and extracts the key fields for you.

**Once you're in**
- See your **Angel Score** — Kevin's AI assessment of your raise across team, market, traction, and pitch quality. Refresh it any time after profile or deck updates.
- See **Kevin's investor matches** — investors aligned with your stage, sector, and country, ranked by fit. Includes both registered investors on the platform and Kevin's curated network of VCs.
- **Chat with Kevin** about your deck, strategy, or which investors to approach first. Kevin has read your deck and knows your context.
- **Connect with investors** — click Connect on any match. When they accept, messaging unlocks both ways.

**What's not built yet**
- Multi-document upload (single pitch deck for now).
- Pro tier (custom AI, custom subdomain, white-label) — coming soon.
- Native mobile app.

## If you're an investor

**Getting started**
- Click your invite link → create your account.
- Build your investor profile: firm name, thesis, sectors you invest in, stages, country, ticket size range.

**Once you're in**
- See **Kevin's founder matches** — founders aligned with your thesis, ranked by fit (and scored by Kevin's Angel Score).
- Open a match → see one-liner, sector/stage/country, full pitch deck access.
- **Chat with Kevin** about specific founders, market segments, or your overall thesis.
- **Connect with founders** — click Connect on any match. When they accept, messaging unlocks both ways.

**What's not built yet**
- Portfolio tracking (coming soon).
- Investment memo generation — limited beta.
- Investor pipeline / watchlist UI — parked, will return after we re-design with input from cohort users.
